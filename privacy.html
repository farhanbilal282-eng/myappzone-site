Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

What we collect

If you create an account we store your email address and, if you sign in with Google, the basic profile details Google shares (name and email). If you use the contact form we store the name, email address and message you send.

How we use it

Account details are used only to sign you in and to let you open apps. Contact messages are used only to reply to you. We do not sell your information.

Storage and security

Data is stored with our database provider, Supabase, with access controls so that only you (for your account) and the site owner (for contact messages) can read it.

Third-party apps

When you press Open or Live Demo you may leave MyAppZone. Each app is responsible for its own privacy practices.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete your account or messages at any time using the contact page.
